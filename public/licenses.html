Stockfish (analysis engine)

Standard chess is analysed with Stockfish 18, running in your browser. The build is Stockfish.js 18.0.8 "lite single-threaded", distributed unmodified.

  • Stockfish © T. Romstad, M. Costalba, J. Kiiski, G. Linscott and other contributors — source code.
  • Stockfish.js © 2026 Chess.com, LLC (Nathan Rugg) — source code, package 18.0.8.
  • Neural network: Linmiao Xu (linrock).

Stockfish is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPLv3): license text. The license gives you the right to copy, change and share Stockfish too. The rest of the site is a separate program that talks to Stockfish only in text commands (UCI). Variants are analysed with punkchess's own engine.
